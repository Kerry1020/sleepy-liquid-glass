# Signed green response of the existing native Slider

This bounded calibration follows the eight-round RGB study. It changes no native control, track, material, gesture, source geometry or production code. Research stays in this asset repository. It is not optical acceptance and does not identify UIKit's internal color pipeline.

## Question and fixed inputs

The existing full100 star pixel at world (192.75,411.25) is (6,100,96). Global input G+20 leaves its R at6 while reducing B by9. A shared saturation followed by channel clipping and a fixed later rim can produce this unequal response. The preceding **unclipped** affine-family rejection therefore cannot reject a clipped family.

Acquire exactly four new rounds, in order: `control-pre`, `Gminus20`, `Gminus40`, `control-post`. Each has the unchanged full100 public held gesture on the original three-color five-point16pt star, then the original unchanged flat128. Gminus20/40 subtract20/40 encoded bytes from G across the entire source PNG. Original G minimum48 ensures no source clipping. R/B, all source coordinates, AA, ICC, six compatibility-only inputs and the flat bytes remain unchanged. Seven-point outputs are not acquired or consumed.

This reuses the existing iOS26.5/23F77 simulator and the exact already-built App/XCTest products from `slider-color-response-20261116`; no build or source patch is required. The legacy test method name and geometry metadata remain historical full100 fixture labels; they do not assert optical100% coverage. The collector's native dependency bytes, native binaries, new inputs, protocol and analysis scripts are bound in a new freeze before capture. Old freezes are never edited.

Evaluate affine and one-dimensional clipped-affine response independently per output channel at endpoint-byte tolerances1,4,8; do not confuse these with prior tolerances on finite differences. R/B are nonincreasing with input G, G nondecreasing. The clipped family is only an expressive necessary-condition screen. A family compatible with four samples does not establish a shared saturation, its spatial support, physical clipping or processing order. In particular, two negative steps that leave R on its floor do not reject an arbitrarily deeper latent floor; reverse recovery supports but does not uniquely establish clipping. Reject interpretations that hide any channel, rim or sign through pooling. No production parameter is adopted from this experiment alone.

No enlargement of inputs, fit domain or tolerances after reading outcomes. Further neutral-source tests, if needed, require a new plan. This study is consumed calibration, not a new independent holdout, and cannot replace the existing colored5/6/8-star transport regressions or later dynamic validation.
